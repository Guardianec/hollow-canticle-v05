# Original material asset

The following records the older fallback presentation. Default v0.5 biome assets
instead come from the original Blender pipeline described below.

- File: `public/assets/weathered-stone.png`.
- Use: shared stone albedo and low-amplitude bump input on independently modeled
  slabs, walls, foundations, and ruins. It is not a scene, screenshot, or backdrop.
- Tool: built-in `image_gen.imagegen`, original generation, opaque PNG.
- Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: tileable 2048x2048 PBR base-color texture for the playable stone surfaces of an original dark fantasy dungeon game, not a scene or screenshot. Create one seamless texture with many small irregular weather-worn slate and sandstone cobblestones, chipped and bevelled edges, very fine pitted granular surface, muted grey blue green stone with subtle brown mineral streaks and a few muted moss flecks inside the fine dark mortar seams. Orthographic straight overhead flat scan, uniform soft ambient illumination, no directional cast shadows, no perspective, no objects, no characters, no text, no glow, no symbols, no borders. Texture should remain a useful tiling material wrapped over independently modeled 3D stones and ruins. Premium painterly realistic stylized material with fine natural surface variation. Not clean square bricks, not cartoon linework, not a complete dungeon scene.

The tool chose its output resolution; the prompt's requested size is preserved here
as provenance. The final PNG is stored inside the project and copied into `dist/assets/`
by Vite. Fabric weave, fungal cap markings, and glow masks are generated by Canvas at runtime.
Molten crust uses an animated procedural cellular shader. Other shapes,
models, animation, and sounds are code-generated. Dependency/font licenses are in
`public/licenses/`.

## Supplied references

`docs/references/current-before.png` is the user's current-state comparison.
The three `target-*.png` files are supplied quality references, with separate roles
for exploration, dungeon structure, and combat. These files are documentation only;
the renderer never imports them and they are not present in the production build.

The three supplied animation, combat, Shinobi, world and UI sheets for v0.7 and
future work, together with the complete original release brief, are preserved in
the [v0.7 reference library](references/v07/README.md). Its index records the
original filenames and explains how to use the archived material.

## v0.5 Blender content

For the v0.7 additions, the existing environment pipeline is retained. Shinobi
adds one separately authored skinned body and two dedicated black cloth/lacquer
PBR families, bringing the runtime inventory to 153 assets and 17 material
families. The hat, mask, asymmetric armour, scarf, katana and sidearm are original
geometry in `tools/blender/characters.py`; none is sampled from the reference
images. See [v0.7 QA](QA-v0.7.md) for the current inventory and review limits.

`tools/blender/` builds 124 environment assets and 13 rigs, each with three LODs,
and fifteen material families. Runtime files live in `public/assets/v05/`; their
manifest links packs, node names, material IDs, dimensions and triangle counts.
The two v0.5 sheets and release brief stay in `docs/references/v05/` as reference.

Base color is baked through an emission connection to isolate albedo, including
metallic materials. Tangent normal, roughness and AO use their respective bake
passes. Geometry AO and stone stains export in COLOR_0. Shared source PNGs remain
in ignored `.cache/bakes/v05/`; `--rebake` refreshes them. Base-color cache version 2
invalidates the earlier diffuse bake. GLB compression preserves UV/color attributes.
Runtime maps are WebP at 1024 and 512 pixels; external textures use glTF's flipY=false
convention. The images are original procedural sources, not sampled reference pixels.

See [visual review](VISUAL_REVIEW-v0.5.md) for inventory, screenshots and art limits.
